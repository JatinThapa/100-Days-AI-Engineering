# Day 81 — Revisiting LLM Evaluation & Real-World AI Applications 🧠🔍

Today was another revision session in my **#100DaysOfAI** journey. Since I was busy with other things, I went back to **Days 26 and 27** and revisited two connected topics: **evaluating LLMs** and **understanding where they create real business value**.

I revised different LLM leaderboards and evaluation platforms such as **Artificial Analysis, Hugging Face, Vellum, SEAL, LiveBench, and Arena.ai**, focusing on why there isn't really a single “best” LLM. The right choice depends on factors like **intelligence, cost, speed, and the requirements of the task**.

I particularly revisited the idea of comparing **intelligence against cost per task**—because a highly capable model isn't necessarily the most practical one when you're processing thousands of requests.

I also refreshed the progression of business AI applications:

**Automation → Augmentation → Differentiation → Agentic AI**

From simple LLM-powered applications to domain-specific systems using **proprietary data, RAG, tools, and eventually autonomous agents**, the real value comes from solving a specific problem better rather than simply wrapping an LLM in a product.

💡 **Today's takeaway:** Building AI systems isn't just about knowing which model is powerful. It's about understanding **which model, architecture, and level of autonomy actually make sense for the problem and the business.**
