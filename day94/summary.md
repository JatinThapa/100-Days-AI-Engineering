Day 94 — 100 Days of AI 🤖

Today was all about revisiting the AI leaderboard ecosystem. I explored the good old AI Arena, checked out Hugging Face’s leaderboards, and looked at charts comparing LLMs across different capabilities and tasks.

The main takeaway: there isn’t one “best” LLM for everything — different models can shine at reasoning, coding, math, vision, speed, or general chat. Understanding these benchmarks helps in choosing the right model for the right job. 🚀

I also came across several other interesting leaderboards worth exploring:

🏆 LMArena / Arena — real-world human preference comparisons

🤗 Hugging Face Open LLM Leaderboard — open-model benchmark comparisons

📊 MTEB — embeddings and retrieval models

💻 BigCodeBench / Big Code Models — code-generation performance

🧠 MMLU-Pro — challenging multi-domain knowledge and reasoning

🦾 GAIA — general AI assistant/agent capabilities

🤖 Agent Leaderboard — LLM performance on agentic tasks

👁️ Open VLM Leaderboard — vision-language models

🎙️ Open ASR Leaderboard — speech-recognition models

🏅 RewardBench — comparing model reward/preference performance

⚡ LLM-Perf — model performance and efficiency

🎯 LiveCodeBench — coding ability on fresh programming problems


Hugging Face currently hosts a surprisingly broad collection covering everything from LLMs and embeddings to coding, agents, vision, speech, and multimodal models. 

Day 94/100 — another revision day, but a useful reminder that model selection is just as important as model building. 🚀
