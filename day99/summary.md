Day 99 — A Tour Through My 100 Days of AI 🚀

Today, on Day 99 of my 100 Days of AI journey, I took a step back and revisited the entire journey so far.

What started with understanding the basics of LLMs, Chat Completion APIs, prompts, and model interaction gradually evolved into much more practical concepts — including RAG pipelines, embeddings, vector databases, chunking strategies, retrieval, reranking, query rewriting, and LLM evaluation.

I also spent a significant part of the journey exploring fine-tuning and model optimization. I worked with models like Llama 3.2 and Qwen, experimented with QLoRA, quantization, 4-bit NF4, LoRA adapters, and Hugging Face Transformers, and learned how these techniques can make larger models much more practical to run.

The journey then moved towards AI agents and agentic systems. I built everything from simple tool-using workflows to more structured agent loops involving planning, tool execution, price estimation, deal discovery, notifications, and orchestration. I also explored Pydantic structured outputs, function/tool calling, agent frameworks, and building agent loops from scratch rather than depending entirely on abstractions.

One of my bigger projects throughout this journey was the AI-powered product price estimation system, where I experimented with RAG, neural networks, a fine-tuned proprietary pricer, ensemble approaches, and eventually wrapped these components into an agentic architecture.

Along the way, I also explored serverless AI deployment with Modal, local vs remote inference, OpenRouter, Ollama, Gradio, W&B, vector databases, and model leaderboards, while repeatedly revisiting older concepts to strengthen my understanding.

Looking back, the biggest change isn't just the number of topics I've covered. It's the shift from learning individual AI concepts to understanding how they fit together into real software systems.

Tomorrow is Day 100. One more day to complete the challenge. 🤖🔥
