## Day 42 Summary – From Inference to Training 🧠

After an intense stretch of learning RAG, I shifted my focus toward traditional data science and machine learning.

Today, I learned the difference between improving models at inference time using prompting, tool calling, and RAG, versus actually training a model by adjusting its parameters using data.

I also explored generalization, transfer learning, and fine-tuning. Since training massive LLMs from scratch is slightly outside my budget by a few million dollars 😭, fine-tuning an existing model with business-specific data is a much more practical approach.

Over the next week or two, I’ll be collecting datasets, preprocessing them, and training prediction models.
